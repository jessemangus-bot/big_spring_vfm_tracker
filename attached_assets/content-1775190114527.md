1\. Participation Requirements

1.1 Expectations

Since this event has gotten so large (also fun and successful), we need everyone's help to keep the event organized so we can continue it in future years. Please be polite and courteous in all your communication. Be patient and help others.

Be on time. There is nothing more frustrating than walking through the room looking for someone, only to find out they are running late or wandering about the hall.

1.2 Date & Location: TBD Don't have final info yet.

\\* Please send your thanks to Jeff Anderson and the BGG.CON team for continuing to allow us to have the VFM for BGG.CON!

\\* I (Matt Freitas, Tolio) will be running the Virtual Flea Market and attending as well as Team Geek members.

\\* We won't be requiring masks but we do recommend masks in this crowded situation. Please remember the general Health & Safety Guidelines: "Respect the personal choices of those around you. This includes the choice to wear or not wear a mask."

1.3 Room Plan

Posted the final week before BGG.CON on the main header. In general (almost) all sellers will have space at a table if they sell roughly 5 games or more.

1.4 Not following through with commitments

Unfortunately, in recent years there are too many sellers/buyers backing out of deals, and it is taking an inordinate amount of time. The VFM is just so big that I cannot be an intermediary to get buyers and sellers to communicate and resolve issues. Sellers must have their games to sell before they list them. Buyers must follow through with commitments which they made months ago even if a lower price is posted. If something unexpected comes up both need to come to some sort of mutual agreement and may require compensation for lost opportunity to sell. Both sides should also notify me of the resolution. If this is not done, I will unfortunately have to ban users from participation in the virtual flea market. If you are contacted by me in geekmail, you need to respond immediately and work things out with the other party to mutual agreement. Buyers who fail to make their committed purchase will be reported to Matthew Monin (Octavian). (This is a very small minority, so if you have not already been contacted by me, then it is not you.) 2026 addendum: In 2025 we had only about 5 problem sellers/buyers out of 420. Do not be that person who causes issues.

1.5 Shipping Games: See the BGG.CON Master list for FedEx delivery information.

1.6 Wednesday Night Meetup

Since BGG.CON does not officially take any hotel space on Tuesday, there can be no official or unofficial events prior to Wednesday.

2\. Buyers Requirements

2.1 Buyers Room Plan

Sellers tables will continue to be listed by BGG username in approximate alphabetical order. Sellers may be rearranged a bit out of order to facilitate dividing up the tables by number of games sold. There will be an alphabetical list of sellers at the door which will indicate the exact row and column of a particular sellers table (e.g. G5). You can look up who you are purchasing from ahead of time, and mark them on your purchase envelopes AHEAD OF TIME. That will help you find the seller quickly and efficiently. Signs on tables will make it easier to find them.

2.2 Buyer Guidelines

2.2.1 All buyers MUST be BGG.CON attendees to participate in the virtual flea market. Team Geek will be at the doors to enforce this, and buyers should not purchase from sellers who are not attending BGG.CON.

2.2.2 Commitments: Buyers and Sellers must follow through with commitments. See Rule 1.5!

2.2.3 Verify before you buy! If you are expecting that special promo to be in a package of games, ASK for a photograph from the seller to PROVE they have exactly what you want before you commit to buy.

2.2.4 Summary of Unsold Games and Request Geeklist - Use one of these links for an alphabetized list of games still available\[/u\]

\- Alphabetical search tool courtesy of Eric Bettan (Carnage994): [https://yulgame.com/Vfm/Items/363504](https://yulgame.com/Vfm/Items/363504)

\- If there is something you are looking for, think about adding it to the BGG.Con Virtual Flea Market Request List.

\[geeklist=TBD\]\[/geeklist\]

2.2.5 Buyers MUST attend the START of the 7 pm hour of exchange and stay until all your games are received. Do NOT be late and make sellers wait for you! Buyers are REQUIRED to either show up, or notify the Seller they will not be there. Do NOT be 'that guy' which makes a seller wait all hour trying to find you. You must find the seller! Buyers stay on the OUTSIDE of the tables for ease of finding games. Buyers may not use hotel luggage carts.

2.2.6 If you are a buyer, bring one envelope for each seller you are buying from. Each envelope should be marked on the outside with the seller's user name, table assignment, and the items you are buying and should contain exact change! After doing this for a few years, we can tell you that this makes the event go much more smoothly for everyone. Put your name on your envelopes so it's easily identified as paid by sellers or in case it gets lost.

2.2.7: Create a spreadsheet with the games you're buying. Create columns for Game Name, Game Cost, Method of Payment, Seller Name, Seller BGG Username, Comments, etc. If you're paying via PayPal, Venmo, etc. make a note. Print off your list prior to the event and bring it with you. Bring bags to haul off your 'new' games. You won't be able to use the luggage carts.

2.2.8 Any attempt by a buyer to deceive the seller and pick up a game which you did not rightfully purchase will be dealt with harshly.

2.2.9 BGG.CON Bazaar Marketplace

The BGG.CON Bazaar Marketplace is a separate event, where you can explore and purchase games at the con. It is not related to the Virtual Flea Market, but please do attend!

3\. Sellers Requirements

3.1 Sellers Room Plan

In general all sellers will have space at a table if you sell roughly 5 games or more. If less than that, you may need to meet individually. I will entertain sellers combining if needed, but you will need to notify me AND tell the buyers to go to that persons username table. Sellers may be rearranged a bit out of order to facilitate dividing up the tables by number of games sold. There will be an alphabetical list of sellers at the door which will indicate the exact row and column of a particular sellers table. There are 80 tables. There will be no ticket to sign up for sellers tables. I will assign a table to each seller up to our capacity. If you do not get a table because you sold too few games, you are on your own to arrange the exchange with the buyer.

3.2 Seller Guidelines

3.2.1 All sellers MUST be BGG.CON attendees to participate in the virtual flea market. Team Geek will be at the doors to enforce this, and sellers should not sell to buyers who are not attending BGG.CON.

3.2.2 Commitments: Buyers and Sellers must follow through with commitments. See Rule 1.5!

3.2.3 Sellers must have their games to sell before they list them.

3.2.4 Please do add your items to this list! You choose how to sell your items: Fixed Price, Auction, Dutch Auction. You should monitor your own items.

3.2.5 Do NOT delete your item. People expect items to remain historically. If you must remove an item, Mark them as sold or no longer available, but do not delete the item.

3.2.6 Any Auctions **must have a stated end date and conclude by May 10th**. Games must be marked sold by May 10th so that we know to assign you a table. I cannot monitor and contact sellers about auction items. If I see an auction without an end date by May 10, I will contact you about it and if it isn't fixed i may simply delete your item from the list. (This deadline does not apply to BIN since you will mark the game sold when it is sold in order to get table space.)

3.2.7 Math Trade

If you participate in the BGG.Con Math Trade, make sure that items are NOT listed in both at the same time. This will not be monitored. If you do so, it is your responsibility to immediately mark items sold as no longer available in the Math trade, and mark them deleted from this list if they are traded. If you make a mistake and sell a game twice, it is your responsibility to fulfill all your commitments, no matter what the cost is to you. It is far better that you avoid conflicts like this.

3.2.8 Sellers should arrange a bulk commission at the BGG Marketplace or individually by game.

3.2.9 Any attempt by a seller to deceive a buyer by significantly miss describing the item (e.g. missing specified expansion) will be dealt with harshly.

3.2.10 Sellers will be allowed into the room to set up between 6-6:45pm on Thursday evening. No games will be allowed into the room after 6:45 pm. Sellers stay on INSIDE of tables. Do NOT block the outside traffic with games! You are expected to be at your sellers table on time for the full hour or until all your games are sold. If you cannot attend the VFM due to unforeseen circumstances, notify me and make that clear to your buyers in your listing. Sellers may not use hotel luggage carts. If you are also a buyer, you must make other time arrangements to do your VFW pickups outside the VFW event, or have another person do your pickups during the VFW. You must remain at your sellers table.

3.2.11 Make sure you take the $$$ out of the Envelopes and throw the envelopes in the trash. Don't leave them for me to clean up!

3.2.12 List Aggregator Tools:

\- If your item is an auction, please include the word "Auction" someplace. (It helps the aggregator tools)

\- If you are selling an item that deserves an alternate name in a summary list (i.e. Misc Game Accessory), please place this code in your item someplace: "+altname+ Detailed Title -altname-"

\- Mark sold items as: SOLD so that the summary tools can remove it from the available list using +sold+. Mark who purchased it as well.

\- If you change your price, please keep the new price on the same line of the geeklist (Do something like $25 $20)

3.2.13 There is an easy way to add items to the geeklist, courtesy of Eric Bettan (Carnage994):

Alphabetical search tool: [https://yulgame.com/Vfm/Items/375812](https://yulgame.com/Vfm/Items/375812)

The link to add games is: [https://yulgame.com/Vfm/AddItems/375812](https://yulgame.com/Vfm/AddItems/375812)

And a new way by Steven Branham (Branhammer) [https://bgg.honormountain.co/sell](https://bgg.honormountain.co/sell)

3.2.14: At the end if you decide to share tables with someone, please geekmail me, and please make it CLEAR: "X is at table Y", not "Y and X are sharing tables but I am not telling you which."

3.2.15: Addition for 2026. This is a big one! We are starting to get reports of counterfeit copies of games, even to the extent of miscellaneous plastic in the box instead of a game. If you as a seller have ANY inkling that your sale might be counterfeit, disclose it ahead of time! A buyer has a right to examine the game received and reject the sale if the game is counterfeit. If your game is in shrink-wrap and the buyer thinks it is counterfeit you as a seller have a choice:

1\. Cancel the sale and keep the shrink-wrapped copy.

2\. Open the shrink-wrap and let the buyer examine the game so that both of you can both decide if the sale should go through.

Tips from Past Sellers:

1\. Create a spreadsheet with the games you're selling. Create columns for Game Name, Game Cost, Method of Payment, Buyer Name, Buyer BGG Name, Buyer Table Assignment, Comments, etc. If you’re paying via PayPal, Venmo, etc. make a note. Print off your list prior to the event and bring it with you. Print out the table assignments beforehand. Circle your table. I recommend using mail merge from the spreadsheet to create Removable Sticky notes on the game with the name of the Buyer picking up your games.

2\. Send out geek mails and confirm with the game sale with people who have bought games from you. Sometimes people miss responses on the geeklist.

Adding Games to the List

The easy way to add items to the VFM geeklist:

1\. Add all your items to your BGG games collection list.

2\. Tag items for trade in your BGG collection. Note: This won't include expansions at this time.

3\. Then use Eric Bettan's (feel free to tip him) posting tool with instructions to add games: [https://yulgame.com/Vfm/Items/375812](https://yulgame.com/Vfm/Items/375812)

4\. Type in your BGG Username and click load.

5\. Your list will be populated.

6\. Simply add notes to the field that says "Message to add on each entry" for example:

Additional Notes:

1\. Let's meet up at the VFM Thursday from 7pm to 8pm.

2\. I accept Cash (exact change in an envelope), Paypal, Zelle, or Venmo.

3\. I am open to discounts on multiple game buys. Please post here and/or send a Geekmail with any questions."

7\. In the "Buy it now" field enter the price of the game.

8\. Then Click "Add Item".

9\. Rinse, wash, and repeat.

Use Item Tags in your listing:

+altname+ Whatever You'd Like To Title Your Item -altname-

The +sold+ tag avoids improperly tagging an item as sold simply because the word "sold" appears in the listing, perhaps in the game name or something.

The tag +remove+ is used to remove an item from the list aggregator tools without actually deleting it from the Geek List.
